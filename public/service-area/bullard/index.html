Why Bullard

Bullard’s rural character and larger lots mean planters get seen from a distance — front-porch pots, gate-entry arrangements, and entertaining patios all benefit from a composition that reads at scale. We design for that.

Past Bullard-area work

A lot of our East-Texas-camp and retreat experience (including our work for Pine Cove) applies directly to properties around Bullard and Lake Palestine. We know how to scale planters for big porches, long walks, and outdoor dining areas.
