Frequently Asked

Residential container garden FAQ

Do you do residential container gardens in Tyler, TX?

Yes — residential container gardens are one of our core services. We work with fine homes across Tyler, Flint, Bullard, Whitehouse, and surrounding East Texas.

Can you design planters for an existing set of pots?

Absolutely. Bring us your existing outdoor planters and we’ll design plant palettes built for your home’s light and style — or recommend new containers if it’s the right moment for an upgrade.

Do you offer seasonal pots as a recurring service?

Yes. Many homeowners enroll in our seasonal planter rotations, so their front-porch planters and patio pots get refreshed for spring, summer, fall, and the holidays — automatically.

What’s the typical price range?

Residential container gardens are priced per project based on the number and size of pots, plant selection, and whether you want ongoing maintenance. Expect a clear, itemized proposal before any work begins.

Ready to refresh your container gardens?

Spring and holiday slots go fastest — reach out early.
