### Flint, TX is part of our regular route

Flint sits just 10–15 minutes from our Tyler studio, and we service it on the same schedule as Tyler proper. That matters for seasonal rotations: when we’re turning Tyler planters from summer to fall, your Flint property is on the same rotation.

### Popular Flint projects

Flint has a great mix of lakefront homes near Lake Palestine and growing commercial corridors. Most of our Flint engagements are residential container gardens — porch planters and patio pots for larger homes — plus the occasional local-business or church storefront install.

- Patio pots and pool-area container arrangements
- Seasonal pots for spring, summer, fall, and the holidays
