### Whitehouse is close to home

Whitehouse is one of the faster-growing communities around Tyler, and we regularly pick up work in and around the ISD area, along Highway 110, and in the newer residential developments. Because we’re already making the Tyler loop, Whitehouse seasonal rotations stay on the same schedule.

### Typical Whitehouse engagements

- Front-porch planter design for newer homes
- Storefront and small-business pots along Highway 110
- Church and school-office entrance container gardens
- Seasonal color and holiday planters

Every Whitehouse planter we install is designed by a trained horticulturist — so the plant selection, sun exposure, and water needs are built for East Texas, not guessed at.
